Architecture begins where noise stops: a gate of cedar burned black, standing in its own weather. The soot is not decoration. It is how a system is engineered to survive a hundred harsh seasons, and the first thing this craft asks you to understand.

Climb the worn steps and the digital sanctuary lifts out of the mist, paper screens lit from inside like lanterns in the mountain cold. Above the eaves a vermilion moon holds its place, patient, half hidden. Nothing here is rushed. Strict taxonomies, deterministic pipelines, and zero-fluff code.
